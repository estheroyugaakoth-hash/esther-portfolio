01. Inclusive Hospital Management System

A healthcare system concept designed to help hospitals manage information while making digital services more accessible to people with different abilities.

The Problem

Some patients face barriers when accessing digital healthcare services, while healthcare providers need organised ways to manage patient information.

My Proposed Solution

  • Clear, accessible page layouts and navigation.
  • High-contrast and adjustable text options.
  • Voice-guided navigation concepts.
  • Separate access areas for patients, doctors, and administrators.
  • A foundation for organised healthcare information management.

Technologies

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL are planned technologies for this project.
